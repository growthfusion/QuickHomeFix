# QuickHomeFix — Meta Ads Copy (Angi Exhibit A Compliant)

Source rulebook: Angi Quality Guidelines (Exhibit A) word list, as pasted into this
project's compliance review. Every line below was checked against all 9 categories:
Incentives/Free Offers, Unsubstantiated Claims, New Product Promises, Special
Program/Government Language, Quiz/Survey, Scare Tactics, Disparaging Language,
Guarantees/Financing/Warranties, and Purporting to Be a Local Pro.

Pixel/tracking setup (Section 6 of the original prompt) is intentionally excluded —
handled separately in `frontend/js/tracking.js`.

## What Was Removed From the Original Draft, and Why

| Removed | Category | Replaced With |
|---|---|---|
| "Free Quote" / "100% Free" / "Free, no-obligation quotes" | Cat 1 — Incentives/Free Offers | "Get a Quote" / "Quick Quotes" / "No-obligation quotes" |
| "Licensed & Insured" / "Licensed and insured contractors" | Cat 2 — Unsubstantiated Claims | "Vetted Contractors" / "Trusted Pros" |
| "Real contractors, real reviews" | Cat 2 — Unsubstantiated Claims (banned word "Reviews") | "Real contractors, real results" |
| "$200 fix ignored for 3 months becomes a $2,000 repair" (fabricated stat) | Cat 2 — Unsubstantiated Claims | General consequence framing without invented figures |
| "Cost me 3x more" (fabricated multiplier) | Cat 2 — Unsubstantiated Claims | "Cost me a lot more" |

## Notes / Judgment Calls (flagged, not resolved by the word list alone)

- Loss-aversion phrasing ("the longer it sits, the more it costs," "gets worse fast")
  reads as consequence framing, not literal scare tactics (Kill/Crime/Hurt/Harm are
  the only banned Cat. 6 words). If Angi's actual account reviewers interpret "scare
  tactics" more broadly than the literal 4-word list, that's outside what this
  document can verify — confirm with your Angi rep before heavy spend if you want
  zero ambiguity.
- This review checked against the word list as pasted into this project, not the
  full signed Exhibit A contract text. If the real contract has additional clauses,
  re-verify against that source.
